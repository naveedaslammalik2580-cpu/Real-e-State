Before you begin

A few useful answers.

What to expect from the free tools and RealtorOS.

Are the calculators free to use?

Yes. The currently published Commission and Mortgage calculators are free to use on this website. This does not describe RealtorOS pricing.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or signup is required for the current calculators. Open a tool, enter your figures and review the result.

How should I use the estimates?

Use them to explore scenarios, not as a lender quote or a final earnings figure. The mortgage estimate excludes taxes, insurance and other housing costs. The commission estimate excludes taxes and costs beyond the fee you enter. Both use USD; check the assumptions on each tool page.

How is RealtorOS different from the free tools?

The calculators answer specific number-based questions. RealtorOS is the flagship CRM for ongoing contacts, deals and follow-ups. Explore its capabilities and access availability.
