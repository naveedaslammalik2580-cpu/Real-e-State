Understanding Real Estate Commission

A sale price is not the same as an agent's earnings. This calculator first finds one stated commission amount, applies your share, then subtracts the transaction fee you enter.

The terms behind the estimate

Sale price
The property price used as the calculation base.
Commission rate
The percentage of that price used to calculate the stated gross commission.
Agent split
Your percentage share of that gross commission.
Transaction fee
The fixed amount deducted from your share in this model.
Worked example

From sale price to your share

Use a $450,000 sale price, a 3% commission rate, a 70% agent split and a $295 transaction fee.

  1. Gross commission: $450,000 × 0.03$13,500.00
  2. Your share: $13,500 × 0.70$9,450.00
  3. After fee: $9,450 − $295$9,155.00

The final amount is before taxes and any costs not entered.

What changes the result?

A higher sale price, rate or agent split increases your share when the other inputs stay the same. A higher fee reduces it dollar for dollar. Rates and splits vary; the example is not a standard or prescribed arrangement.

What is not included?

The model does not automatically combine both sides of a transaction or deduct taxes, referral payments, brokerage expenses or other business costs. A fee larger than your share can produce a negative result.

Understanding Mortgage Payments

This calculator estimates a fixed monthly principal-and-interest payment for an amortizing loan. It spreads repayment over the entered term while applying the same interest rate throughout.

The terms behind the estimate

Principal
The amount borrowed: home price minus down payment.
Down payment
The portion of the home price paid upfront in this model.
Interest rate
The entered annual percentage rate of interest, divided by 12 for the monthly calculation. Other loan costs are not modeled.
Loan term
The number of years over which the model repays the loan; years × 12 gives the monthly payment count.
Worked example

From home price to monthly payment

Use a $400,000 home price, an $80,000 down payment, 6.5% annual interest and a 30-year term.

  1. Principal: $400,000 − $80,000$320,000.00
  2. Monthly rate: 6.5 ÷ 1,2000.00541667 (rounded)
  3. Payments: 30 × 12360 months
  4. Estimated monthly principal + interest$2,022.62

The calculation uses the unrounded monthly rate; displayed currency rounds to cents.

See the payment formula

M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1).

P is principal, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments. At 0% interest, the calculator uses M = P ÷ n. Total paid is M × n; total interest is that amount minus principal.

What changes the result?

With other inputs unchanged, a larger down payment reduces principal and a higher interest rate increases the payment. At a positive fixed rate, a longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest in this model.

What is not included?

The result excludes property taxes, homeowner insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA fees and lender costs. It does not model rate changes, extra payments or loan-specific fees. It is not a lender quote or the full monthly cost of owning a home.
