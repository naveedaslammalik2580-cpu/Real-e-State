How NOI is calculated

Start with the annual rent, subtract a vacancy allowance and add other income to get effective gross income. Subtract every operating expense — property tax, insurance, maintenance and repairs, the management fee and HOA and other costs — and what remains is the net operating income. The management fee is a percentage of effective income, so it moves with the rent you actually collect, not the rent you hope for.

Why lenders and investors use it

NOI is the one number that lets you compare two properties on the asset alone, no matter how each deal is funded — financing never appears in it. Divide NOI by the price and you get the cap rate; divide it by annual debt service and you get DSCR, where most lenders look for 1.25 or better. The expense ratio (expenses divided by effective income) shows how much of every collected dollar the property eats before it pays you anything.

Important note

This is an informational estimate, not investment advice. It ignores appreciation, tax, principal paydown and resale costs, and a single year of rent and expenses is not a guarantee of the next. Verify rents, occupancy and costs with local records before making any buying decision.

Key takeaways

  • NOI equals effective gross income minus every operating expense. Mortgage, depreciation and income tax are excluded.

  • The expense ratio shows how much of every collected dollar the property eats before it pays you anything.

Frequently asked questions

What is NOI in real estate?

Net operating income: the income a rental property produces after operating expenses, before mortgage payments, depreciation and income tax. It measures the property as an asset on its own.

How is NOI calculated?

Annual rent minus a vacancy allowance, plus other income, gives effective gross income. Subtract property tax, insurance, maintenance, management and HOA and other costs, and what remains is net operating income.

How is NOI different from cash flow?

Cash flow subtracts the mortgage payment as well. NOI stops before financing, so it can be compared across properties and funding structures; cash flow cannot, because it depends on the loan.

How does NOI relate to cap rate and DSCR?

Cap rate is NOI divided by price. DSCR is NOI divided by annual debt service. Both start from NOI because it is the property’s income before financing.

Why does the management fee change with vacancy?

Because it is a percentage of effective income, the rent actually collected after vacancy, not the full rent roll. A higher vacancy therefore lowers both income and the management fee.

Is NOI a guarantee of future income?

No. It is an informational estimate, not investment advice. A single year of rent and expenses is not a guarantee of the next year. Verify rents, occupancy and costs with local records.

Limitations and next steps

This estimate ignores appreciation, tax, principal paydown and resale costs. Verify rents, occupancy and costs with local records before making any buying decision.
