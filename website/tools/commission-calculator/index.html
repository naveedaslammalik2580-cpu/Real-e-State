How the commission estimate works

Gross commission = sale price × commission rate. Your share = gross commission × your split percentage. The final displayed amount subtracts the transaction fee you entered, but not taxes or any additional business costs.

Who is this for?

Agents estimating income from a sale, team members considering commission splits, and brokers checking a simple example scenario.
