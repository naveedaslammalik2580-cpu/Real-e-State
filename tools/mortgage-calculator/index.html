How the payment is calculated

Monthly principal and interest follow the standard amortizing formula: M = P × r(1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments. Property tax and insurance are spread evenly across 12 months; HOA dues are added monthly; PMI is charged only while the balance is above 80% of the price.

Understanding the estimate

Your actual monthly housing cost can differ due to tax reassessments, insurance premiums, HOA changes, PMI cancellation rules and lender fees.

Important note

This is an informational estimate, not a lender quote or financial advice. Verify terms with your lender before making any borrowing decision.
